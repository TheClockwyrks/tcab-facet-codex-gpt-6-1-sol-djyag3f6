Facet is a gem puzzle about the stones you leave behind. Swap neighboring gems into lines of three or more, watch them shatter, and let the next fall reveal another opportunity. Each of the seven stones has its own color and cut silhouette.

Every clear puts pressure on its neighbors. Fine cracks spread into fractures, then leave a stone flawed. Touch that stone with another clear and it breaks too, paying double and carrying the fracture farther across the board. A worn corner can become the beginning of a spectacular chain.

Longer lines leave something precious. Four gems earn a brilliant that takes its surrounding ring; crossing lines create a star that sweeps a row and column. Five or more leave a many-colored prism: trade it against a stone to break every gem of that kind.

Each fall can begin another clear, climbing the chain multiplier as the board gives way. Reach the level target, take stock of your best move, and open a fresh field. Keep finding productive swaps: when none remain, your round is over.
